## Structure

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–4 s | Claim: the next big thing isn't an AI bottleneck, it's quantum computing |
| 4–10 s | Stakes: could be 10× bigger than AI |
| 10–16 s | Why now: a new hardware cycle before the AI boom finishes |
| 16–34 s | Explainer: bits (0 or 1) versus qubits (0 and 1 at once) |
| 34–39 s | Consequence: many possibilities at once |
| 39–48 s | Proof: Google's chip did in 5 minutes what would take a supercomputer 10 septillion years |
| 48–54 s | Kicker: the parallel-universes idea (David Deutsch) |

Style: polished, static camera, presenter on camera in medium framing, moderate editing pace, educational tone, original audio, follow call to action.

## What to copy

1. **Lead with a contrarian "next thing" claim** in the first line and on screen at the same time.
2. **Stack stakes, then explanation, then proof.** The surprising number arrives in the second half and pays off the hook.
3. **Keep the frame simple** (one presenter, static camera) so the on-screen text carries the structure.

**Avoid:** a long, dense explainer without a concrete proof point. The 39-second "10 septillion years" moment is what makes the explanation shareable.

## Brief for your own version

> Hook line: "Everyone's watching [obvious trend]. The real one is [your contrarian pick]." On-screen in frame 1: "The next [category] to watch". Beats: claim → stakes (one big number) → 15-second plain-language explainer → one concrete proof → kicker that invites comments.
